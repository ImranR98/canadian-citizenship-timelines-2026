This website is a personal, non-commercial informational project. We value your digital privacy and do not use invasive tracking technologies or cookies.

We use two minimal methods to ensure our website is secure and functioning properly:

These logs are stored temporarily and are automatically deleted.

Privacy-Friendly Analytics: We use Plausible Analytics to understand our overall website traffic (such as counting total page views). Plausible does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and never stores your IP address. All data is completely anonymized and aggregated.

2. Sharing and Retention

We do not sell, rent, or share any visitor data with third parties or advertisers. We do not build user profiles. Data collected by our server logs is only used for server maintenance and is overwritten automatically.
